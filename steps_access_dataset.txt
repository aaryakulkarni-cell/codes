1. If you upload the dataset directly into Google Colab
Suppose your teacher gives you uber.csv during the practical.
Open your Google Colab notebook.
Click the folder icon on the left.
Click the upload icon.
Select uber.csv from your computer.
Wait for the upload to finish.
Then write:

import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv("/content/uber.csv")

df.head()


2. If you upload the dataset to Google Drive
If you put uber.csv inside your Google Drive's MyDrive folder, then use:

from google.colab import drive

drive.mount("/content/drive")

import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv("/content/drive/MyDrive/uber.csv")

df.head()

3. What about VS Code Jupyter?
If your notebook and CSV are in the same folder, write:

import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv("uber.csv")

df.head()
